# Marinated Chicken
**Source:** My strange brain

Serves|Prep Time|Marinade Time|Cook Time
:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:
$n$|2 mins|1 day|40m

Item|Quantity
:--|--:

## Method
0) Place chicken strips in a freezer bag or similar
0) Coa chicken in a small amount of oil 
0) Add 'dry' spices and coat chicken
0) Add sauce
0) Leave overnight in the fridge
0) Bake in oven
