# "Nando's Chicken"
**Source:** My strange brain

Serves|Prep Time|Marinade Time|Cook Time
:-:|:-:|:-:
1|2 mins|1 day|40m

## Ingredients
Item|Quantity
:--|--:
Chicken Breast Strips|1 breast
Chilli Powder|some
Chilli Flakes|some
Smoked Paprika|some
Nando's Sauce|some

## Method
0) Place chicken strips in a freezer bag
0) Add 'dry' spices and coat chicken
0) Add sauce
0) Leave overnight in the fridge
0) Decant and bake in oven


## Serve
- With Mash
